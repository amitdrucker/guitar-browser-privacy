Guitar Browser privacy policy

Updated: 23 October 2026. Developer: amit drucker.

Guitar Browser lets you browse websites, including Tab4U and Ultimate Guitar. It has no app account, app backend, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or cloud synchronization.

Bookmarks and script preferences are stored on your device. Android WebView stores cookies, cache and website storage locally, including website login sessions. The application disables Android backup. Removing the app removes its local application data.

Websites receive requests you make through the browser, which can include your IP address, browser information, search queries, cookies and information you submit directly. Text searches use Google. Websites can display advertising or process data according to their own policies. The app developer does not receive browsing history through an application server. This is not a promise about the websites' data collection.

Clear browsing data, available in the browser menu, removes cookies, website storage, cache, form data and browsing history and signs you out of websites. It retains bookmarks and per-site script preferences. Remove bookmarks with the page bookmark button. Clear all app data in Android Settings to remove all application preferences and bookmarks.

The bundled Tab4U 4.8 and Ultimate Guitar 0.04 scripts and the authored Shironet, Negina and Nagnu readers execute locally on supported websites and ship with app updates. They transform displayed song content and add reader controls without an application data upload facility. There is no native JavaScript bridge or remote script update mechanism.

The only requested Android permission is Internet access. Cleartext HTTP, mixed content, file/content access and third-party cookies are blocked. Website processing and Internet browsing are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Contact: amitdrucker@gmail.com.

Website accounts and subscriptions are managed by the website provider. Use that provider’s account deletion controls to remove its account or server data. Website retention policies govern information already sent to websites. The developer does not sell browsing data.
